常见问题

我的数据存在哪里？

保存在你的设备上。如果登录了 iCloud，还会同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有空间，开发者无法查看。旗迹没有自己的服务器。

换手机或重装后，记录还在吗？

在新设备上登录同一个 iCloud 账户并打开旗迹，记录会自动同步回来。也可以通过 iCloud 或电脑备份整机恢复。

怎么导出我的数据？

设置 › 数据 › 导出本地 JSON。

怎么删除数据？

在 App 里删除赛事、轮次或球场，会同时从设备和 iCloud 删除。要删除全部数据：删除 App 会清除本机数据；iCloud 中的副本可以在 iOS 设置 › [你的名字] › iCloud › 管理账户储存空间 › 旗迹 中删除。

提示「无法读取之前的数据」怎么办？

原文件已经完整保留，不会被覆盖。登录 iCloud 的话，记录通常会自动同步回来。如果没有回来，请在 设置 › 数据 中导出保留的文件，发邮件给我们协助恢复。

智能教练会把我的数据发到网上吗？

不会。教练模型内置在 App 中，完全在设备上运行。

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

On your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it also syncs to your own private iCloud storage, which the developer cannot access. PinTrace has no servers of its own.

Will my records survive a new phone or a reinstall?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on the new device and open PinTrace; your records sync back automatically. A full iCloud or computer backup restore also works.

How do I export my data?

Settings › Data › Export local JSON.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a tournament, round or course in the app removes it from the device and from iCloud. To delete everything: deleting the app removes on-device data, and the iCloud copy can be deleted in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › PinTrace (旗迹).

The app says it couldn't read my previous data.

The original file has been kept intact and will not be overwritten. With iCloud, your records usually sync back on their own. If they don't, export the kept file from Settings › Data and email it to us so we can help recover it.

Does Smart Coach send my data online?

No. The coach model is built into the app and runs entirely on your device.
