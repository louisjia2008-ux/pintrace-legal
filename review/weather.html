WeatherKit attribution — screen recording

Recorded on a physical iPad running PinTrace 1.0 (33), the build submitted for review. The app's language is Chinese; the times below show where the Apple Weather mark and the legal link appear.

Where WeatherKit data appears

Every place that shows WeatherKit data shows the Apple Weather trademark (the Apple logo followed by “Weather”) with a link to the legal attribution page (link text: “天气数据来源与法律声明”, “Weather data sources and legal”).

  1. 0:17 New round screen: the weather card shows the reading, the Apple Weather mark and the legal link.
  2. 0:22 Tapping the legal link opens Apple's WeatherKit data sources and legal page.
  3. 0:33 Scoring screen: the weather strip shows the same mark and link.
  4. 0:37 Hole weather sheet (tap the strip): the mark and link at the bottom of the sheet.

Weather is fetched after the player allows location access while creating a round. Without weather data, no weather or attribution is shown.
