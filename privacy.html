旗迹 PinTrace

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日 · English version below

支持与反馈

概要

  • 旗迹没有账号系统，也没有开发者自己的服务器。
  • 你的高尔夫数据保存在你的设备上；如果你登录了 iCloud，还会同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有空间，开发者无法查看。
  • 旗迹不收集、不出售、不共享你的任何数据，不做广告、不做用户追踪，也不包含任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。

1. 你在旗迹里记录的数据

你录入的赛事、轮次、每洞成绩、每杆记录、球场、球包、训练记录和设置，都保存在本设备上。我们（开发者）无法访问这些数据。

2. iCloud 同步

如果你的设备登录了 iCloud，旗迹会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 把上述数据同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，用于在你的多台设备之间同步和换机恢复。私有数据库只有你的 Apple ID 能访问，开发者看不到其中的内容。iCloud 数据的处理适用 Apple 的隐私政策。如果没有登录 iCloud，数据只保存在本机。

3. 位置

只有在你使用 App 时，并在你授权后，旗迹才会获取位置，用于：

  • 天气：通过 Apple 的 WeatherKit 获取本轮天气和风向。获取天气时，位置会发送给 Apple。
  • 每杆位置与球场足迹：记分时保存每杆的大致设备位置，作为你记录的一部分保存在本机（以及你的 iCloud 私有空间）。你可以在 设置 › 隐私 中关闭这个功能，或删除某一轮已记录的位置。
  • 球场搜索：搜索球场时使用 Apple 地图。

4. 麦克风与语音识别

语音记分功能使用麦克风和 Apple 的语音识别把你说的话转成文字。设备支持离线识别时在本机完成；不支持时，音频可能由 Apple 的服务器处理（适用 Apple 的隐私政策）。旗迹不保存录音，只把识别出的文字作为记分或备注保存在你的记录中。

5. 相册

旗迹只申请「添加到相册」权限，用于把你导出的记分卡图片保存到相册，不会读取你的照片。

6. 旗迹 Coach

复盘与训练建议由 App 内的统计规则在你的设备上计算，不使用 AI 模型。你的数据不会发送到任何服务器。

7. 数据的导出与删除

  • 在 App 中删除赛事、轮次或球场，会同时从你的设备和 iCloud 私有空间中删除。
  • 在 设置 › 数据 中可以导出本机数据（JSON）。
  • 删除 App 会删除本机上的全部数据。要删除 iCloud 中的副本，请前往 iOS 设置 › [你的名字] › iCloud › 管理账户储存空间，找到「旗迹」并删除数据。

8. 儿童

旗迹不向任何人收集个人信息，包括儿童。

9. 政策变更

本政策适用于当前版本的旗迹。将来如果加入账号、云端功能或其他需要处理你数据的服务，我们会在功能上线前更新本政策，并在 App 内说明、征得你的同意。

10. 联系我们

如有任何隐私问题，请发邮件至 louisjia2006@gmail.com。

PinTrace (旗迹)

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Summary

  • PinTrace has no accounts and no servers operated by the developer.
  • Your golf data is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it also syncs to your own private iCloud storage, which the developer cannot access.
  • PinTrace does not collect, sell or share any of your data, shows no ads, does not track you, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

1. Data you record

Tournaments, rounds, hole scores, shots, courses, your bag, practice records and settings are stored on your device. We (the developer) cannot access them.

2. iCloud sync

If your device is signed in to iCloud, PinTrace uses Apple's CloudKit to sync this data to your own private iCloud database, so it stays in sync across your devices and can be restored on a new one. Only your Apple ID can access the private database; the developer cannot see its contents. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud. Without iCloud, data stays on the device only.

3. Location

Only while you use the app, and only with your permission, PinTrace uses your location to:

  • Weather: fetch round weather and wind from Apple WeatherKit. Your location is sent to Apple for this.
  • Shot positions and course footprint: save the approximate device position of each shot as part of your records (on the device and in your private iCloud). You can turn this off, or delete the positions recorded for a round, in Settings › Privacy.
  • Course search: search for courses with Apple Maps.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

Voice scoring uses the microphone and Apple speech recognition to turn what you say into text. Recognition runs on the device when supported; otherwise audio may be processed by Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. PinTrace does not keep recordings; only the recognised text is saved in your records as scoring or notes.

5. Photos

PinTrace only asks for add-only access to save exported scorecard images. It never reads your photos.

6. PinTrace Coach

Review and practice suggestions are calculated on your device by the app's own statistics rules. No AI model is used, and your data is never sent to any server.

7. Exporting and deleting data

  • Deleting a tournament, round or course in the app deletes it from your device and your private iCloud.
  • Settings › Data lets you export your on-device data as JSON.
  • Deleting the app removes all data on the device. To delete the iCloud copy, go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, find PinTrace (旗迹) and delete its data.

8. Children

PinTrace does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes

This policy covers the current version of PinTrace. If a future version adds accounts, cloud features or any other service that processes your data, we will update this policy before that feature ships, and explain it and ask for your consent in the app.

10. Contact

For any privacy question, email louisjia2006@gmail.com.
